Gynaecology Care

Women's Health & Wellness

Overview: Gynaecology at Confident Hospital focuses on the diagnosis, treatment, and care of the female reproductive system, offering both general and specialized women's health services.

Services Offered: Menstrual disorder treatment, PCOS/PCOD management, infertility evaluation, fibroid and cyst management, menopause care, and routine gynaecological check-ups.

Advanced Procedures: Laparoscopic and hysteroscopic surgeries, colposcopy, cervical cryotherapy, and uterine fibroid removal.

Expert Team: Our experienced gynaecologists and women’s health experts provide personalized care tailored to every stage of a woman’s life, from adolescence to menopause.

Facilities: State-of-the-art labour rooms, fetal monitoring systems, ultrasound & Doppler scanning, and 24/7 emergency care.

At Confident Hospital, our Gynaecology Department is dedicated to improving the overall health of women through compassionate care, cutting-edge technology, and a patient-first approach.

When to See a Gynaecologist: Regular check-ups, abnormal bleeding, pelvic pain, pregnancy planning, postnatal care, and menopause-related concerns are all valid reasons to consult our specialists.

Preventive Care: We emphasize preventive screenings like Pap smears, breast examinations, and HPV vaccinations for long-term health and early detection of serious conditions.

Why Choose Confident Hospital? Our commitment to quality care, privacy, comfort, and professional expertise makes us a trusted choice for women across all age groups.
