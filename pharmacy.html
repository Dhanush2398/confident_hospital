Pharmacy Services

24/7 In-House Medical Store

Overview: Confident Hospital houses a fully functional, 24/7 in-house pharmacy that provides easy access to a wide range of genuine, quality-assured medicines and healthcare products.

Available Medicines: Prescription drugs, over-the-counter (OTC) medications, surgical items, nutritional supplements, diabetic care, mother & baby products, and emergency medications.

Quality Assurance: All medicines are sourced from verified suppliers and stored under controlled conditions to maintain efficacy and safety.

Expert Guidance: Experienced pharmacists are available to provide medication counseling, dosage guidance, and patient education on drug interactions and usage.

Integration: Seamlessly integrated with our hospital's treatment protocols, ensuring timely delivery of medicines during inpatient care, surgeries, and emergencies.

Our pharmacy at Confident Hospital is committed to ensuring patients receive the right medicines at the right time. With a focus on reliability, availability, and affordability, we support better treatment adherence and health outcomes.

Why In-House Pharmacy Matters: Eliminates delays, ensures accurate prescriptions, and enhances convenience for both inpatients and outpatients.

Home Delivery & Support: We also offer home delivery services for elderly and chronic care patients. Our pharmacists are available for follow-up consultations.

Why Choose Confident Hospital? Round-the-clock availability, genuine medicines, personalized attention, and fast service make our pharmacy a trusted extension of our patient care system.
